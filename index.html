Good To Know

Questions, answered.

How much space and power do you need?

About a 10' × 10' area for the booth, backdrop and prop table, plus a standard outlet within 25 feet. We'll sort the details with your venue ahead of time.

How do I hold my date?

Send us an inquiry with your date and we'll confirm we're free. A retainer holds it, and the balance is due a week before the event. Dates go first come, first served.

Do you travel outside Houston?

We do. Anything within 30 miles of Houston is included — Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Spring, Cypress and more. Past that we add a small travel fee and tell you upfront.

Can the booth go outside?

Yes, as long as it's covered, level, near power, and out of rain and direct sun. We'll help you pick the right spot.

How do guests get their photos?

Right away — they text it to themselves or scan a QR code at the booth. Afterward you get a link to the full gallery with every photo, GIF and boomerang from the night.

What's idle time?

Idle time is when the booth is set up but paused — during dinner or the ceremony, say. It's a cheaper way to have everything in place early without paying the full hourly rate.
