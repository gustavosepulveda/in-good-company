Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How much space and power do you need?

We need roughly a 10' × 10' area for the booth, backdrop, and prop table, plus one standard power outlet within 25 feet. We coordinate details with your venue before the event.

How do I reserve my date?

Send an inquiry with your event date and we'll confirm availability. A retainer locks in your date, with the balance due one week before your event. Dates are first come, first served.

Do you travel outside of Houston?

Yes! Travel within 30 miles of Houston is included in every package — Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Spring, Cypress, and more. Beyond that, we add a small travel fee quoted upfront.

Can the booth be set up outdoors?

Absolutely, as long as there's a covered, level area with access to power and protection from rain and direct sun. We'll help you pick the perfect spot.

How do guests get their photos?

Instantly! Guests text the capture to themselves or scan a QR code right at the booth. After the event, you receive a link to the full online gallery with every photo, GIF, and boomerang.

What is idle time?

Idle time is when the booth is set up but paused — for example, during dinner or a ceremony. It's a great way to have the booth ready early without paying full hourly rates.
